Retail & e-commerce

An online fashion store

The problem

Shoppers ask about stock, size, price, shipping, and COD day and night. Messages wait after hours, and returning buyers are treated like strangers. Product info sits in catalogs, spreadsheets, and templates.

How Conversa solves it

The customer bot answers stock, size, price, shipping, and COD from your catalog and live stock data on the website widget and WhatsApp, day and night. Once a shopper shares a phone number, their history follows across channels. Tricky cases reach an admin with the full chat; a knowledge-gaps report shows what it could not answer.

Manufacturing · Odoo

A factory with Odoo ERP

The problem

Buyers ask about products, MOQ, and lead times. The owner, sales, and finance wait on exported reports: orders, stock, invoices, and profit & loss, one export at a time.

How Conversa solves it

A customer bot answers buyers from spec sheets, certificates, and the product catalog: material, dimensions, MOQ, lead time, and which distributor covers their city. On staff WhatsApp/Telegram numbers, a personal assistant reads live Odoo records and answers in plain language: sales orders, stock, invoices, CRM leads, profit & loss, and tax summaries. Reads are read-only by default; changing anything needs write access switched on in Odoo, and it proposes the change first, then waits for your yes.

What's live today

Customer bot from documents Personal assistant on WhatsApp/Telegram Live Odoo reads Read-only by default Proposed writes + your yes

Public services

A clinic, a campus, or a licensing office

The problem

Most of the queue is information and paperwork: what do I need, how much is it, when are you open, how do I register. Asked hundreds of times.

How Conversa solves it

The assistant answers requirements, fees, schedules, locations, and procedures from the official documents the office uploads: PDF or photos. It walks people through registration, checks documents against the checklist, and picks up where a returning person left off, on any channel. Anything needing judgement goes to a named officer in the live inbox.

Answers from official documents Registration walkthrough & document check Cross-channel memory Handoff to a named officer
